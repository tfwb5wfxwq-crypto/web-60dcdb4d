Data Deletion Instructions

“LDVK” app · last updated November 10, 2026

To have any data about you processed by the “LDVK” app deleted (a comment or message you sent to the Instagram account @ludovik, your Instagram-scoped ID or username), send an email to ludovik@influxcrew.com with your Instagram username.

Your data is deleted within 30 days and you receive a confirmation by email.

Without any request, identifiers used by the automatic reply are deleted automatically after 12 months (30 days for comment IDs), and copies of comments and messages after 12 months at most.

You can also remove an app's access to your account at any time in Instagram: Settings › Apps and websites.
