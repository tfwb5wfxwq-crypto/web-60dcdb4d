Purpose

“LDVK” is a private app operated by LDVK SAS (SIREN 834 994 352, Paris, France) that lets Ludovik manage his own Instagram professional account @ludovik: read the comments and messages received, and send one automatic reply to people who ask for a link with a keyword. It is not offered to the public and no sign-up is possible.

Use

The app complies with the Instagram Terms of Use and the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies. It only sends a private message in response to a person who first commented on a post of @ludovik or wrote to the account, at most once per person, and never sends unsolicited or promotional messages.
