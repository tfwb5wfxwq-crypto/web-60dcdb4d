Privacy Policy

“LDVK” app · last updated November 10, 2026

Who we are

The “LDVK” app is a private tool operated by LDVK SAS (SIREN 834 994 352, 22 rue de la Folie Méricourt, 75011 Paris, France). It is used only by Ludovik, the company's owner, to manage his own Instagram professional account @ludovik. It is not offered to the public and has no user sign-up.

What the app does

  • Reads the media published by @ludovik, and the comments and direct messages received on that account.
  • When someone comments a keyword announced in a post (for example “REELS”) or sends that keyword by direct message, it replies once: one short public reply under the comment and one private message containing the requested link.
  • Shows the owner the comments and messages received on his account so he can read and answer them.

Data processed

Only data that Instagram makes available to the owner of the account: media of @ludovik, comments and direct messages sent to @ludovik, the Instagram-scoped ID and public username of their authors, and timestamps. No data is collected about other accounts or about people who never interacted with @ludovik.

How it is used

This data is used only to answer the comments and messages sent to the account, and to make sure each person receives the automatic reply only once. It is never sold, rented, shared with third parties or used for advertising or profiling. The app never sends a private message to someone who did not comment or write first.

Storage and retention

The automatic reply keeps only technical identifiers: the author's Instagram-scoped ID and a hashed (non-readable) form of the username, for 12 months, and the comment ID for 30 days, to avoid duplicate replies. Comment and message text is not kept by the automatic reply. Copies of comments and messages shown to the owner are kept on his private server for 12 months at most, then deleted. Data is hosted on Cloudflare (Workers KV) and on the owner's private server; access tokens are stored encrypted and never published.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to access or delete data about you (see Data deletion), or write to ludovik@influxcrew.com. Requests are handled within 30 days.
